Research abroad · Summer 2023

Mérida, Yucatán

Yucatán Initiative, Introduction to Research Abroad Program

20.97° N, 89.62° W

A Texas A&M research program run with the State of Yucatán. My project compared public and personal transportation in fast-growing cities: Mérida and Valladolid in Yucatán against Austin and San Antonio in Texas, framed around UN Sustainable Development Goal 11.

The program also took us through about 20 research labs, including UADY, UNAM, CICY and CIATEJ.

About

I was born in Karachi and grew up in Houston, about ten minutes from Johnson Space Center. During COVID, my younger brother and I started a small business helping people online plan and build their own PCs. Most of that work was listening to what someone wanted and turning it into a parts list, and that is still a large part of what I do at internships.

Since then I've interned at Southwest Airlines, NASA, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, JPMorgan Chase, Microsoft and SpaceX. I also spent a semester at Texas A&M's campus in Qatar and did field research in Mérida, Mexico.

Outside of work, I'm into squash, pickleball, skydiving, reading and chess.
